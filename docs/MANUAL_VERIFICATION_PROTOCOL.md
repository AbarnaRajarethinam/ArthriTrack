# ArthriTrack Manual Verification Protocol

Use this protocol only with informed participants and non-identifying test profiles. Do not record video. Export/delete the test profile after the session. Camera estimates remain experimental.

## First-time usability

Observe without coaching first. Record confusion, abandoned attempts, recovery steps, taps/clicks and participant wording; do not invent scores.

1. Complete onboarding with keyboard only, then at 200% and 400% zoom. Verify Back, readability preference, consent explanation, focus visibility and no hidden controls.
2. Ask the participant to select their comfortable hand, movement and target. Confirm they can explain what will be measured and that opposition targets mean individual finger attempts.
3. Grant camera access. Test front and rear cameras where available. Record whether displayed anatomical left/right matches the actual hand. If it does not, stop hand-specific testing and file a blocking defect.
4. Test too far, too close, partially outside frame, two hands, uncertain palm orientation, dim light and camera disconnection. Confirm only detectable conditions produce guidance and green readiness disappears when engine inputs are rejected.
5. Run fist, spread and opposition calibration. Include comfortable restricted movement. Confirm phases advance because start/moved/return positions occurred, interruptions preserve trustworthy completed phases, timeout offers recovery, and insufficient evidence never becomes a result.
6. Record each movement. Interrupt tracking mid-cycle, pause/resume, background/restore the browser and stop the camera stream. Confirm completed cycles remain, incomplete cycles restart, timer terminates, and tracks release after finish/cancel/navigation.
7. Review results. Ask the participant to explain capture sufficiency, movement amplitude, speed, repeatability and limitations. Confirm they do not interpret these as arthritis severity or clinical accuracy. Test Save, repeated Save, network failure/retry, Retry Assessment and Return to Dashboard.
8. Verify the saved record on dashboard, progress history, JSON export and appointment report. Confirm units/version are present and deletion removes the assessment from retained report drafts while noting the deletion.

## Accessibility checks

- Keyboard: logical route/form order, visible focus, all actions available, no keyboard trap.
- Screen reader: page headings, field labels, camera/calibration status announcements, buttons and progress meaning. Ensure high-frequency frame status is not announced continuously.
- Reflow: 320 px, 390 px, tablet and desktop widths; 200%/400% zoom; large system text.
- Visual: WCAG contrast review for default, warning, focus and disabled states.
- Motor access: touch targets, slider alternatives, accidental activation, time pressure and ability to pause.
- Motion: operating-system reduced-motion preference removes demonstrations without hiding instructions.

## Measurement reliability study preparation

Define the measurand and reference method with qualified clinical/methodology collaborators before recruitment. Prespecify device/camera, posture, comfortable endpoints, repetitions, rest, failure criteria and algorithm/model versions. Collect repeated same-session and different-session measures across representative devices, lighting, skin appearances, hand shapes and comfortable movement ranges. Compare per-joint/task estimates with an appropriate reference; report bias, agreement limits, repeatability, failed-capture rate and exclusions. Do not infer pain, inflammation, arthritis severity or clinical improvement. No accuracy or minimum-detectable-change claim is permitted until this work is complete.
